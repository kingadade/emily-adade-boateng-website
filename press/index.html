Short biography

Emily Adade Boateng is a Ghanaian gospel singer and songwriter from the Western Region. Born in 2012, she began recording at eleven and has since released three albums, two EPs and five singles. She won Best New Artiste of the Year and Gospel Song of the Year at the 2025 Western Music Awards, and Discovery of the Year at the 2026 Western Gospel Awards.

Full biography

Emily Adade Boateng was born in 2012 in Ghana’s Western Region and started recording at eleven. Her debut album, King of Glory, arrived in 2024, followed the same year by the Aseda EP, whose title track features Joyce Blessing. Ahead of Ghana’s December 2024 elections she released Peace with Selina Boateng, a plea for a calm nation that was referenced in Parliament during the election season.

Miracle Working God (June 2025) and Nyame Prɛkopɛ (January 2026) made it three albums before her fourteenth birthday. At the 2025 Western Music Awards she won both Best New Artiste of the Year and Gospel Song of the Year, the latter for Aseda. In March 2026 she signed with PM Entertainment Consult as the Takoradi company’s first gospel artiste. The single Defender followed in July 2026, and in October she released On My Way, a five-song EP on which she wrote every song. In November 2026 she was named Discovery of the Year at the 6th Western Gospel Awards.

Emily writes her own songs and sings in Twi and English, moving between praise, worship and jazz-inflected arrangements. Beyond the stage she ministers in prisons, on school and university campuses, and in orphanages.
