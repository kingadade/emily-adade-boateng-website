# Content questions for Emily (and family)

One sitting, phone voice-notes are fine — I only need honest sentences,
not polished ones. Twi answers are welcome; note them and we'll present
both Twi and English.

## 1. Her voice on the songs  (highest priority)
For each song, ONE or TWO sentences from Emily herself. Prompts:
- What was happening in your life when this song came?
- What do you want someone to feel when they hear it?
- Is there a line in it that means the most to you? Why?

Songs to cover (any order, skip any she has nothing to say about):
Defender · Glory · Nyame Prɛkopɛ · W'afi · Ayeyi · Aseda · Peace ·
Fakye · Miracle Working God · Okofo Nyame · Thankful Heart · Yen Boafo

## 2. Ministry stories  (second priority)
One real story each — a specific day, a specific person if possible
(no real names needed for prison stories):
- PRISON: A moment from one visit that stayed with her. What did she sing?
  What happened in the room?
- CAMPUS: One school visit — which school, roughly when, what did the
  students do or ask?
- ORPHANAGE: One child or one moment she still remembers.
Also: roughly how many visits has she made to each? ("about 6 prison
visits since 2024" is enough — no precision needed.)

## 3. Lyrics
Which 3–4 songs should have full lyrics on the site first?
For Twi songs, English translations — even rough ones. We'll credit a
translator if someone helps.

## 4. Events
- Any confirmed or half-confirmed dates for the rest of 2026?
- Recurring appearances (monthly service, annual concert)?
- Where does she most want to perform next year? (usable as a line even
  without dates)

## 5. One extra (optional but powerful)
A 30–60 second phone video of Emily saying hello to visitors of her own
website — face to camera, one take, no script. Goes at the top of the
Story section.
